# Interview guide — engaged couples

**Status: not done.** No interviews have been run. Do not cite this file as user research.

The phase 0 exit includes a small, diverse set of conversations with potential users. This script is the plan for that work. Results, if they are collected later, belong in a new decision-log entry. They do not belong in the app, in analytics, or in the question JSON as sample answers.

## Who to invite

Talk with engaged couples, or with each partner separately when that is safer and more comfortable. Aim for differences in age, culture, faith, income, and whether the couple already lives together. Include people who might skip a sensitive question, not only people who enjoy structured conversations.

Stop an interview if the person is distressed. Do not continue into a topic they want to leave. This interview is not counseling.

## Consent

Before starting, say:

- Participation is voluntary and can stop at any time.
- The conversation is about the product idea, not a request to settle a real relationship decision.
- They should not share secrets they would not want written in notes.
- Notes record reactions to the product. They do not record a couple's private answers to the practice questions.
- Notes are stored outside the app repository if they contain anything personal.

## What to ask

1. Which conversations have you postponed, and what made them hard to start?
2. Would you want to answer a question privately before reading your partner's answer? Why?
3. What would you need to see on the screen before you were willing to submit an answer?
4. If you could see that your partner had answered, but not their words, would that feel fair or uncomfortable?
5. After a hard conversation, what would you want to find again a month later?
6. What would make you skip a question or mark it "not ready"?
7. What should this app refuse to do, even if a couple asked for it?
8. Would you come back for a second conversation, and what would that second visit need to include?

Listen for whether a journal is something they would reopen, or only a log they would never read. That is the retention question this phase needs.

## What to record

For each conversation, record the date, the person's own description of their situation in broad terms (for example "engaged, living apart"), and short notes on the eight questions. Record doubts and refusal as clearly as interest.

Do not record names in the product repository. Do not paste a participant's real answer to a relationship question into `engaged_questions_v1.json`.

## After the interviews

Update the decision log with what changed: audience, question wording, sharing rules, or the disconnect rule. If the interviews do not happen, the phase 0 exit stays open on this item even though the written boundaries exist.
