# Safety and high-risk topics

**Status:** Draft product policy. A specialist safety review has not been done. Do not treat this file as a clinical protocol or a crisis-service design.

## Role of the app

Couple Conversations helps people practice a structured conversation. It is not an emergency service, a domestic-abuse assessment, or a counselor. The app says that limit on the home screen.

## Skip and "not ready"

Every question can be skipped or marked "not ready to discuss". There is no score and no penalty. High-sensitivity questions say this in the question guidance as well as in the controls.

The first catalog marks these questions as high sensitivity:

- Whether you want children
- Faith, culture, and traditions

A high-sensitivity screen tells the person they can skip, and that they do not owe the app or their partner an immediate answer.

## Confrontation

The app does not tell a person to confront their partner. It does not suggest surveillance, pressure, or forced disclosure. A skip is a complete choice.

If a later AI feature sees content about immediate danger, threats, abuse, coercion, or fear of retaliation, it must:

- stay with the person who wrote the text
- avoid suggesting a joint confrontation or an automatic disclosure to the partner
- encourage contact with local emergency services when the person may be in immediate danger
- avoid claiming confidentiality the product does not actually provide
- avoid presenting the app as a crisis service

## Local resources

A launch country is not chosen. The app tells people to contact local emergency services if they are in immediate danger. It does not show a hotline number, a charity, or a clinic. Those may be added only after they are verified for the launch place.

## Content review

Questions are open-ended, specific, and written without a "correct" relationship. They avoid shame, scoring, and the assumption that every couple wants the same family, faith, or division of work.

`reviewStatus: draft` means the questions are ready for the prototype and for later interviews. They are not approved for a public launch. Sensitive topics, especially abuse, coercive control, and personal safety, need review by someone qualified in relationship safety before release.

## Prototype behavior

The practice app repeats that people should not type real relationship information. Nothing in this phase is sent to another person or to a server.
